Terminals are the dark-rose nodes. The table shows dp[v][S] for every node v (rows) and every non-empty subset S of terminals (columns). Cells fill in as the algorithm proceeds: base cases first, then subset merges, then full-graph shortest-path relaxations for each subset size.
